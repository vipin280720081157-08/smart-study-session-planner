Transparent Priority Formula

Priority is computed deterministically in R using: Score = (Urgency × 50%) + (Difficulty Weight × 20) + (Daily Demand × 2.5) where Urgency scales with days remaining, Difficulty uses multipliers (Easy: 1.0x, Medium: 1.5x, Hard: 2.0x), and Daily Demand measures needed study hours divided by eligible days before the exam.

System Rules & Algorithmic Principles

1. Strict Exam Boundary

A subject is never scheduled on or after its exam date. Preparation sessions strictly take place in the window before exam day.

2. Daily Hour Ceiling

Total scheduled sessions for any single day will never exceed your designated daily study limit, protecting against burnout.

3. Multi-Pass Allocation

Time is distributed in balanced 1.0 to 2.0 hour study blocks across active subjects, prioritizing nearest exam deadlines first.

4. Transparent Feasibility

When available days and daily capacity are mathematically insufficient to cover estimated hours, remaining hours are flagged explicitly as an unscheduled deficit.
